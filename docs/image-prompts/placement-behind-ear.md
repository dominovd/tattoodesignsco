# Behind ear tattoo gallery image prompts

Generation mode: built-in image generation, default quality.

Shared direction: photorealistic vertical 3:4 editorial close-ups of one adult model's ear, hairline, side neck and the skin directly behind the ear against a neutral studio background. Hair must be swept away so the tattoo remains visible. Every tattoo must look healed, stay small in scale and fit the narrow anatomy. Use realistic ear anatomy, natural skin texture and soft directional light. Exclude earrings, piercings, jewelry, text, letters, numbers, logos and watermarks.
