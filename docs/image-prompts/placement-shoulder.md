# Shoulder tattoo gallery image prompts

Generation mode: built-in image generation, default quality.

Shared direction: photorealistic vertical 3:4 editorial photographs of one adult model's shoulder, upper arm and a small part of the upper torso against a neutral studio background. Every tattoo must look healed, remain clearly visible and follow the rounded deltoid anatomy. Use natural skin texture, realistic anatomy, a tasteful crop and soft directional studio light. Exclude jewelry, text, letters, numbers, logos and watermarks.
